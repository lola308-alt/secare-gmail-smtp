SECARE Gmail SMTP Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Overview

SECARE Gmail SMTP is a personal automation tool used by the owner to send weekly research-scan emails from the owner's Gmail account.

Information and Data

The application does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Gmail OAuth credentials are stored locally in Windows Credential Manager. The application does not store the user's Gmail password in the application workspace.

Google Account Access

The application uses Google OAuth to obtain permission to send email through Gmail SMTP. The user can revoke this access at any time through their Google Account.
